### Phase 1: Research Foundation (Literature Review)

**Overview:** In this phase, we conducted a rigorous analysis of the current State-Of-The-Art (SOTA) in privacy-preserving federated dataset matching. We identified critical vulnerabilities in standard Differential Privacy (DP) Locality-Sensitive Hashing (LSH), specifically that noise insertion degrades retrieval utility unacceptably for medical datasets. We proposed Topological Data Analysis (TDA) as a macroscopic privacy layer to decouple structure from sensitive point-cloud data.

**Goal:** Establish the SOTA in privacy-preserving dataset matching.

**Status:** Completed.
- [x] Identified current SOTA limitations (Information leakage in standard DP-LSH).
- [x] Proposed Topological Data Analysis (TDA) as a macroscopic privacy layer.
- [x] Documented the structural mutual information vulnerabilities in existing literature.
