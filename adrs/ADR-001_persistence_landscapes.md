# ADR-001: Persistence Landscapes vs Persistence Images

> **Historical material — not current validation evidence.** Retained for design
> context, contributor attribution and provenance. Phase labels, figures and
> capability statements below are not current ATLAS claims. See the
> [current evidence and historical-material guide](../docs/HISTORICAL_MATERIAL.md).

## Status
Accepted (Phase 1 evaluation)

## Context
To execute cross-silo topology matching (Phase 4 / Phase 5), organizations must convert the shape of their local dataset into a mathematical fingerprint that can be shared without leaking the underlying raw data. Persistent Homology yields a Persistence Diagram, but diagrams are difficult to hash, compare, and feed into machine learning models directly because they are multi-sets of points without a natural vector space structure.

We need to transform the Persistence Diagram into a functional or vector representation that supports Locality Sensitive Hashing (LSH) for the Topology Interchange Protocol (TIP).

## Alternatives Considered
1. **Persistence Landscapes:** A sequence of functions that encode the topological features, mapping diagrams into a Banach space.
2. **Persistence Images:** A 2D vector representation generated by overlaying a Gaussian distribution over the points of a persistence diagram.
3. **Betti Curves:** A simple count of topological features at a given filtration threshold.
4. **Persistence Entropy:** A single scalar measuring the Shannon entropy of the persistence diagram.

## Decision

**Primary TIP Signature:**
Persistence Images

**Reason:**
Superior robustness across tested noise levels (EXP-002) and confirmed through hyperparameter sensitivity sweeps (EXP-003) where optimally tuned Images achieved 98.44% hash agreement at 20% noise compared to 84.27% for Landscapes.

**Supporting Signature:**
Persistence Entropy

**Purpose:**
Ultra-fast coarse filtering due to minimal memory footprint (0.2 KB) while retaining strong stability.

**Rejected:**
Persistence Landscapes

**Reason:**
High noise sensitivity under tested conditions. Because landscapes encode absolute boundaries of topological features, they fail to absorb random perturbations effectively, even after hyperparameter tuning.

**Confidence:**
High (Tested synthetically across robust configurations).

**Pending:**
Cross-domain validation (EXP-004) and Privacy leakage testing (EXP-007).

## Evidence
Results from `EXP-001` Topological Signature Benchmark on 5 synthetic datasets (100-200 points, 64-bit LSH):

| Representation | Stable | Collision | Runtime | Memory | Hash Agreement |
| -------------- | ------ | --------- | ------- | ------ | -------------- |
| Landscape      | 96.88% | 0.00%     | ~0.05s  | 15.6 KB | 0.97           |
| Image          | 91.56% | 10.00%    | ~0.05s  | 15.6 KB | 0.92           |
| Betti          | 98.44% | 10.00%    | ~0.05s  | 7.8 KB  | 0.98           |
| Entropy        | 98.75% | 0.00%     | ~0.05s  | 0.2 KB  | 0.99           |

## Can this decision be revisited?
Yes, before TIP RFC-0001 is finalized.
