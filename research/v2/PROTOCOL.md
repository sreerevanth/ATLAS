# Manifold-Core V2 preregistration

Written before any V2 relevance evaluation. V1 is diagnostic only, never a tuning
set. Its 1,500 sampled questions, their support titles, and normalized duplicate
questions are excluded from the new HotpotQA sample. The protected hash inventory
is checked at every stage. No historical PR is part of this investigation.

## Data and split

Seed 20261006. HotpotQA official dev-distractor file, pinned in the baseline,
is partitioned by connected components of shared support titles into internal
development / validation / final-test pools (25/25/50 percent of components).
Seeded sampling takes 200/200/400 questions respectively. These are internal
held-out tests, NOT the official hidden test or fullwiki task. Component IDs and
all exclusions are recorded. MuSiQue answerable v1.0 dev, pinned mirror SHA256
15fa63794d18a94ce12411aca6e2327e65b6e83b0b1490efab3f1962e48abf3b, supplies 300
transfer-test questions after excluding normalized question and support-title
overlap with all selected HotpotQA and V1 questions. No MuSiQue tuning.

Each dataset uses a pooled corpus of the sampled questions' distractor contexts.
All methods share this corpus, encoder, queries, and title-deduplicated ranking.
Corpus construction is transductive: final context text enters the unsupervised
index, never final relevance labels. This does not demonstrate inductive corpus
generalization. MiniLM pretraining contamination cannot be excluded. Exact
title identity defines Hotpot support; MuSiQue uses exact normalized paragraph
content IDs because multiple paragraphs can have the same title.

Encoder: all-MiniLM-L6-v2 revision c9745ed1d9f207416be6d2e6f8de32d1f16199bf,
normalized 384-dimensional CPU vectors, batch64, four threads, max length256.
No answer generation, no EM/F1 answer-quality claim. Retrieval metrics:
all-support@5 primary; support recall@2/5/10, all-support@2/10 and MRR@10 secondary.

## Fixed method matrix

Exact semantic top100 plus one-hop graph expansion from top5, degree16 or32.
Graph score: maximum cosine(seed, candidate) * max(0, cosine(query, seed)),
excluding a candidate's self-edge. Candidate features are standardized within
query, preserving semantic ordering at zero added weights. Graph coefficients
0.1, 0.3; graph-zero expansion is a correctness control. Density-only and raw
V1-style density/persistence scores are diagnostic controls, not V1 retuning.

Topology features: longest finite H1 lifetime, or negative Euclidean distance
between H1 landscapes of query-local and candidate-local neighborhoods. Fixed
landscape grid [0,2], resolution32, two layers; local size16/32. Exact local VR
and greedy landmark8/16 (where smaller than neighborhood). Topology weights
0.05/0.15; sign-reversed and shuffled feature controls. Topology-only and graph
plus topology are both evaluated. The matrix is generated by code, with every
cell reported; no omission of losing cells. Exact local topology is the default.

HNSW isolation uses seed17/29/43 and efSearch16/64/128; both candidate and
neighborhood recall are measured against exact search. Fixed graph32 weight0.1
is compared under exact/ANN neighborhoods on development only.

## Selection and single final evaluation

Development selects one graph configuration by primary mean, then selects a
graph+topology configuration holding that graph degree and weight fixed
(ties: lexicographic method ID). Validation evaluates these
unchanged. Graph enters primary only if its paired cluster-bootstrap 95% lower
bound versus cosine is positive AND gain >=0.02. Topology enters primary only
if the same conditions hold versus selected graph. Otherwise primary falls back
to the simpler accepted method. No post-validation hyperparameter edits.

The selection JSON is committed before final evaluation. One final invocation
evaluates cosine, selected graph, selected topology-only ablation, selected
graph+topology, and primary together on HotpotQA internal final and MuSiQue.
An exclusive sentinel rejects a second invocation. A crash leaves the sentinel;
recovery requires a documented incident, never silent deletion.

## Uncertainty and interpretation

Paired cluster bootstrap (5,000 draws, seed20261006) resamples shared-support
components; report question-bootstrap as a sensitivity analysis, question SD,
win/loss/tie counts and paired exact McNemar p-values (latter assumes independent
questions). Final confirmatory comparisons graph-cosine and topology-graph on
two datasets use Bonferroni 98.75% CIs (four comparisons). All other comparisons
are exploratory. Difficulty subgroup is specified without outcome labels:
Hotpot bridge questions with semantic top1-minus-top5 margin <0.10; MuSiQue
questions with >=3 decomposition hops. Subgroup claims require replication;
no retrospective subset shopping. Narrow failure to reject is not equivalence.

## Topology mathematical and resource gates

Clean/noisy circle and two separated clusters must pass before real topology.
Compare exact VR with greedy landmark VR (NOT a witness complex) on identical
prefixes N250/500/1000; sweep32/64/128 landmarks. Approximation scaling extends
to2000/5000/10000 if corpus permits. Child-process RSS ceiling1.5GiB,
available-memory floor1GiB, wall-time cap120s per cell. No exact runs beyond1000.
Report finite-diagram bottleneck/Wasserstein, essential counts separately,
runtime, peak sampled RSS, dominant H1 lifetime, and available edge/simplex counts.
Local16/32 with landmark8/16 fidelity is measured separately.

Investigate genuine GUDHI weak witness complexes (landmarks32/64, N250/500/1000)
as a research alternative, not a retrieval retrofit. Its squared-distance
relaxation filtration is NOT directly comparable to a VR distance filtration;
do not manufacture diagram-distance guarantees between these axes. Report
counts/runtime/features on its own scale and synthetic validation. Any failed
gate stops downstream use of that topology family.

## Hostile audit and claim boundaries

Verify protected bytes, split/question/support overlaps, provenance, every
matrix cell, locked selections, single final invocation, no post-test selection,
and encoder contamination caveat. Track all code and compact raw ranking/metric
artifacts; generated tables/plots come exclusively from them. Large public text
and embeddings remain reproducible ignored caches with checksums. No production,
clinical, regulatory, privacy, security, or general retrieval-superiority claim
follows from these experiments. A negative result retires persistent topology
from the V2 primary path, not from its explicitly experimental module.
